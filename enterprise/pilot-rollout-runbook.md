## 2. Pilot Ring Architecture & Timelines

### Ring 0: IT & Security Engineering Pilot
- **Cohort Size**: 50 selected endpoints (IT Operations, Security Engineering, QA Automation).
- **Duration**: 72 continuous hours (3 business days).
- **Primary Objectives**:
  1. Validate co-existence with host EDR agents (CrowdStrike Falcon, SentinelOne).
  2. Validate DLP agent compatibility (ensuring extension canvas draws and storage writes are not flagged as data exfiltration).
  3. Validate SSL/TLS deep packet inspection under Zscaler Internet Access (ZIA) / BlueCoat proxies.
  4. Verify MDM policy auto-ingestion from `chrome.storage.managed` upon profile sync.
- **Pass Criteria (Gate 0)**:
  - Zero endpoint crashes or blue/black screens.
  - Zero DLP policy violations generated by CineGlow storage or canvas events.
  - Zero certificate pinning or SSL handshake aborts under enterprise proxy inspection.

### Ring 1: Cross-Departmental Business Champions Pilot
- **Cohort Size**: 500 endpoints distributed across departments:
  - Finance & Accounting (100 endpoints)
  - People Operations / HR (100 endpoints)
  - Global Operations & Supply Chain (150 endpoints)
  - Enterprise Customer Support (150 endpoints)
- **Duration**: 5 full business days (120 hours).
- **Primary Objectives**:
  1. Measure real-world latency overhead across corporate SaaS portals (Workday, Salesforce Lightning, SAP Fiori, ServiceNow, Jira, Microsoft 365).
  2. Confirm absence of host-page CSS styling bleeding or DOM interference.
  3. Verify audio equalizer and volume booster stability during corporate video learning portals.
- **Pass Criteria (Gate 1)**:
  - Zero Sev-1 (Critical Business Impact) incident tickets logged at IT Service Desk.
  - Zero Sev-2 (Major Workflow Degradation) tickets logged.
  - Cumulative Sev-3 (Minor / Feature Query) tickets < 5 total.
  - Host application latency degradation strictly < 50ms (empirical baseline 0ms).

## 4. Emergency Kill-Switch & Rollback Procedure

If a Sev-1 incident occurs (e.g. unexpected browser freeze or core portal conflict):
1. **Remote Kill-Switch Activation (Immediate - Latency < 5s)**:
   - Push `"KillSwitch": true` via Google Admin Console or GPO registry (`HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Policies\Google\Chrome\3rdparty\extensions\cineglow_pro_youtube\policy`).
   - CineGlow Pro service worker captures `storage.managed.onChanged`, halts all background network egress, unregisters dynamic content scripts, and broadcasts `AURA_EMERGENCY_KILL_SWITCH` across all active browser tabs.
   - All 16 content scripts immediately detach observers, unmount canvases, remove DOM elements, and restore original player playback states.
2. **Policy Removal / Force-Uninstall (If required)**:
   - Remove `cineglow_pro_youtube` from `ExtensionInstallForcelist` in GPO / Intune.
   - Chrome Enterprise uninstalls the extension binary silently within 15 minutes of policy sync.
